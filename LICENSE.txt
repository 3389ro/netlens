ADDITIONAL USE NOTES (informational, not part of the licence above)

NetLens is a LAN inventory utility. It is not a vulnerability scanner. The
"Risk" column reports exposure hints derived from open-port heuristics and
must not be relied on as the sole control for any security audit or
compliance assessment.

You are responsible for the legality of your scans. Run NetLens only against
networks you own, administer, or have explicit written authorisation to scan.
Unauthorised network scanning may be illegal under your local computer-misuse
or cybercrime laws.

Trademarks and brand names that appear in scan results are property of their
respective owners. They are derived from the publicly available IEEE OUI
